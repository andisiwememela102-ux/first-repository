Hobbies

Playing Soccer

When I was younger, I enjoyed playing soccer with my friends after school. I remember spending hours outside trying to score goals.

One of my favourite memories was playing soccer with my friends on weekends. We did not always have a proper field, but we still had a lot of fun.

Riding My Bicycle

I also enjoyed riding my bicycle around the neighbourhood when I was a child. I remember riding with my friends until it started getting dark.

Sometimes we would race each other down the road. Those afternoons were some of my favourite childhood memories.

Toys

Toy Cars

I loved playing with toy cars when I was younger. I used to make roads around the house and race my cars.

I remember collecting different toy cars and choosing my favourite one whenever I wanted to play.

Building Blocks

Building blocks were another toy that I enjoyed playing with. I used to build houses, towers and different shapes.

Sometimes my buildings would fall down, but I would just start building them again. It was always fun to see what I could create.
